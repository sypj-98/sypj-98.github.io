Hi this is Yipengjing Sun. I am a PhD student in Faculty of Computing, Harbin Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. [Shengping Zhang](https://homepage.hit.edu.cn/zhangshengping). I am currently a research intern at Ant Group. My research focuses on **human-centric 3D vision & graphics**, including human reconstruction, animation and relighting.
